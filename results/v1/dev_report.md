# Stage 2 dev results (walk-forward, 2004-2015 validation years)

Generated by `python -m src.v1.report`. Shock `mps_bp` (official-method Fed surprise), target = industry minus market return on the announcement day, 12 Ken French industries, scheduled FOMC meetings. Four expanding-window folds: train from 1994 to 2003 / 2006 / 2009 / 2012, validate on the next 3 years. **The 2016+ test set is locked and was not used.**

`r2_oos` > 0 means better than each industry's historical mean. The fair bar is the **zero** row (+0.0106): a model only adds information if it beats predicting 0.

## How to read this

- Effects are small by nature: on a Fed day, the surprise explains a few percent of how an industry moves relative to the market. Positive, significant out-of-sample r2 of 1-3% is a meaningful result in this literature.
- Stage 1 (2y-yield proxy, SPDR ETFs) found nothing beyond the zero forecast; compare.
- Interpretation needs agreement across seeds and folds, not one formula.
