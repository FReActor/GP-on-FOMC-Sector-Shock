## Notes for reading formulas

- gplearn's division is protected: x/y returns 1 when |y| < 0.001. surprise_bp is a whole number of basis points and is exactly 0 on 9% of events, so a formula like `c/surprise_bp` predicts a fixed +1% on those days. That is an artifact, not economics.
- Per-ticker models have 93 training events each; with 5 inputs GP can fit noise easily. Agreement across seeds (section 1) is a better guide than any single formula.
